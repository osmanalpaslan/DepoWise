## Q1. Purpose, target users and why each product is comparable to Alpnex

### Takeaway
No product covers Alpnex's whole scope. Each comparable covers a slice:
- **Heavy-equipment maintenance (CMMS):** HCSS Equipment360, B2W Maintain.
- **Mixed-fleet telematics plus maintenance:** Tenna, Clue, EquipmentShare T3, Samsara.
- **General fleet maintenance:** Fleetio.
- **Construction ERP with equipment costing:** Viewpoint Vista Equipment Management (EM).
- **Small-tool and equipment custody ("zimmet"):** ToolWatch/AlignOps, ShareMyToolbox, Hilti ON!Track.

Only Vista EM, as part of a full ERP, combines the equipment fleet with job costing, inventory, payroll and GL, which is closest to Alpnex's integrated ERP positioning. None of the products found advertises an offline-first desktop client; their offline support is mobile-app only.

### Cited Findings
- **Tenna:** a construction asset-management platform. Its modules cover asset tracking (sites, maps, geofences, utilization, analytics), maintenance (preventive maintenance, work orders, parts, mechanic time cards, cost codes), inspections and compliance (custom inspections, DVIR, IFTA, ELD), tool tracking (TennaQR, TennaBLE), OEM telematics/AEMP and ERP integrations, plus Asset Financials, Schedule and Load Tracking [MKT] — [Tenna home](https://www.tenna.com/)
- **HCSS Equipment360:** a heavy-equipment maintenance system for contractors that "tracks equipment status, maintenance, utilization and overall performance," with full machine history on one screen [MKT] — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360). It belongs to the HCSS fleet line alongside separate products: HCSS Telematics, Fuel Management (FuelerPlus) and Dispatcher [MKT/SNIP] — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360); [Informed Infrastructure](https://informedinfrastructure.com/?p=89670)
- **Equipment360 user ratings [SEC]:** Capterra shows 4.1/5 overall from 19 reviews, with value for money at 2.8/5 — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.ca/software/140169/equipment360)

### Inferences
- **Two ways to split the market:**
  - **Equipment CMMS** (Equipment360, B2W Maintain, Fleetio): deep maintenance, little custody.
  - **Tool custody** (ToolWatch, ShareMyToolbox, ON!Track): deep check-out, transfer and requests, light maintenance.
- Alpnex has both (maintenance plus zimmet), so it competes with combinations of these products. In the US, a contractor typically runs Vista or HCSS for ERP/maintenance plus ToolWatch for custody, and the vendors integrate with each other.
- Vista EM is the closest structural analogue to Alpnex, since equipment costs and revenue post to jobs inside the same ERP.

### Gaps
- **Align vs ShareMyToolbox:** the brief calls the product "Align (ShareMyToolbox)." The fetched AlignOps page does not mention ShareMyToolbox, so the ownership or merger relationship is **(unverified)**.
- **Clue:** no help center was fetched, so all Clue content is marketing-level.
- **Pricing:** not researched (not requested).

## Q2. Modules, screens, fields, buttons and end-to-end flows

### Takeaway
The most detailed verifiable workflow documentation comes from Fleetio (meters, work orders, fuel), Vista (named EM programs) and Hilti ON!Track (alerts, transfers). The recurring flow patterns are:
- **Meter-driven PM:** due-soon and overdue alerts, then a work order, then a completion meter that resets the reminder.
- **Field defect/request to work order:** a request from an inspection or the mobile app becomes a work order in a few clicks.
- **Equipment usage rates posted to jobs:** internal equipment rental charged to the job.
- **Pick tickets and requisitions** for moving tools from a warehouse to a site.
- **Fuel imports** from dispensers or fuel cards, with anomaly voiding.

### Cited Findings

**Meter readings (hours/km)**
- **Fleetio meter model [DOC]:**
  - Each vehicle has a **Primary Meter** and an optional **Secondary/Auxiliary Meter**, enabled via "Edit Vehicle Settings" → "Secondary Meter". Example uses: a crane's upper and lower units, or a generator on a truck.
  - Units are Miles, Kilometers or Hours. Hours display as "Hour Meter".
  - Every entry is stored in **Meter History** (per vehicle and fleet-wide), with CSV export.
  - **Bulk Meter Management** is available to owners and admins.
  - A reading not updated for over a month is highlighted with "Date of Last Update", and a missing reading shows in red.

  — [Fleetio Meter overview](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/meter-overview)
- **Fleetio validation rules [DOC]:**
  - A new reading cannot be lower than any existing or past value.
  - A backdated reading must fall between the readings dated before and after it.
  - **"Mark as Void"** lets an out-of-sequence reading be saved without validation.

  — [Fleetio Meter overview](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/meter-overview)
- **Fleetio meter-entry sources [SNIP]:** Quick Add menus, Vehicle Overview, manual update, linked records (inspection, fuel, service, work order), and integrations/imports (GPS, fuel card, meter-reading import) — [Fleetio meter entry add/update](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/meter-entry-add-or-update)
- **Fleetio work-order meters [SNIP]:**
  - Work orders carry Issue Date, Start Date/Start Meter and Completion Date/Completion Meter.
  - The completion date and meter feed the automatically generated Service Entry, which resolves outstanding Service Reminders.
  - If the start meter becomes out of sequence because of interim usage, the user is prompted to update it or Mark as Void.

  — [Fleetio Work Order Meter Validation](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/work-order-meter-validation); [Fleetio Add Work Orders](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/add-work-orders)
- **B2W Maintain meter settings [SNIP]:**
  - Meter readings are captured when requests are created.
  - Each meter category has settings for **"Exclude from field logs"**, **"Exclude from work orders"** and **"Require a meter reading to complete a work order"**.

  — [B2W Maintain Feature Overview 2023 PDF](https://www.b2wsoftware.com/wp-content/uploads/B2W-Maintain-FeatureOverview_April2023.pdf); [Trimble B2W Maintain](https://www.trimble.com/products/b2w-software/maintain-equipment-maintenance)
- **Vista meter programs [DOC]:**
  - **EM Meter Readings** records odometer and hour readings and updates the EM Equipment record and the Meter Readings (EMMR) file.
  - **EM Company Parameters** controls whether meters are updated during transaction posting. Parts and fuel usage postings can update meters.

  — [Trimble Vista help](https://help.trimble.com/doc/vista/vista/job-resources/equipment-management)

**Preventive maintenance scheduling**
- **HCSS Equipment360 [MKT]:**
  - PM is scheduled by meter readings (hours or mileage) from telematics or manual field readings, or by set dates.
  - Cost codes, part requests, documents and checklists attach to PM services automatically.
  - Alerts convert to work orders "in three clicks".

  — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360)
- **B2W Maintain [SNIP]:**

  - **PM templates** (company or manufacturer schedules) can be applied to many machines at once.
  - The next due point is calculated in one of two modes. **Planned Completion** counts from when the previous interval triggered. **Actual Completion** counts from when the work was actually done.

  — [Trimble B2W Maintain](https://www.trimble.com/products/b2w-software/maintain-equipment-maintenance); [Trimble PM planning blog](https://www.trimble.com/en/blog/construction/article/preventive-maintenance-planning-scheduling)
- **Vista [DOC]:**
  - **EM Standard Maintenance Groups** store "Last Done" meter readings used to decide when service is due.
  - Work orders can be generated automatically from items due for maintenance, or entered manually.
  - **Component tracking** follows the location and costs of components such as engines and transmissions.

  — [Trimble Vista help](https://help.trimble.com/doc/vista/vista/job-resources/equipment-management)
- **Tenna [MKT]:** intervals are set by "utilization, time, or custom triggers" and driven by engine hours or mileage from Tenna GPS. Customizable notifications fire when service is due, and PM alerts "can automatically trigger a maintenance request" — [Tenna Preventive Maintenance](https://www.tenna.com/construction-equipment-maintenance/preventive-maintenance/)
- **Samsara [DOC/SNIP]:** PM schedules are set "By date", "By odometer" or "By engine hours" — [Samsara KB: Create PM schedule](https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405865435789-Create-a-New-Preventative-Maintenance-Schedule); [Samsara KB: Get started with PM](https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/23159418525709-Get-Started-with-Preventative-Maintenance-Schedules)

**Work-order lifecycle, mechanics and parts**
- **HCSS Equipment360 [MKT]:**

- **Tenna [MKT]:** separate modules for Work Orders, Parts, Mechanic Time Cards and Cost Codes. ## Q3. Background processes: telematics/meter import, alerts, approvals, audit, mobile/offline, integrations, roles, import/export

### Takeaway
The dominant background process is **telematics hour/odometer import**: OEM feeds or the AEMP / ISO 15143-3 Asset API supply hours, location, fuel and fault codes, which drive PM automatically. The other common processes are:
- Configurable alerts that convert into work orders.
- Daily fuel-card imports.
- Gateway or BLE auto-location with suggested transfers.
- Audit trails on custody transactions.
- ERP/accounting sync for job cost.
- Mobile offline capture (Tenna, Clue).

### Cited Findings
- **Tenna OEM/AEMP data [MKT]:**
  - The **Asset API follows ISO/TS 15143-3** (AEMP).
  - Imported fields: asset ID, make, model, serial, VIN/PIN, operating hours and idle hours, latitude/longitude, fuel level and consumption, DEF level, and fault codes.
  - Named partners: Caterpillar (VisionLink, My.Cat), John Deere (JDLink), Komatsu (Komtrax), Volvo (CareTrack), Takeuchi, JCB (LiveLink), Vermeer, Develon, TESMEC, Liebherr, Sunbelt Rentals, Bobcat and Link-Belt.
  - Refresh frequency is not stated; TennaCANbus is described only as "higher frequency than OEM alone".

  — [Tenna OEM Telematics & AEMP](https://www.tenna.com/integrated-construction-software/oem-telematics-aemp-integrations/)
- **Samsara Caterpillar integration [DOC/SNIP]:** pulls location, engine hours, fuel level, odometer and fault codes via VisionLink using a "Caterpillar AEMP 2.0" marketplace app, with a documented AEMP 1.2 → 2.0 upgrade. John Deere JDLink provides location, hours, fuel and fault codes — [Samsara KB: Caterpillar VisionLink](https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041465972-Integrate-with-Caterpillar-VisionLink); [Samsara KB: John Deere JDLink](https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038382352-Integrate-with-John-Deere-JDLink)
- **B2W Maintain telematics [SNIP]:** "amplifies the value of AEMP-compliant telematics" to trigger PM. A Samsara marketplace listing says B2W pulls engine hours and meters from Samsara — [Trimble B2W Maintain](https://www.trimble.com/products/b2w-software/maintain-equipment-maintenance); [Samsara marketplace: B2W](https://www.samsara.com/resources/marketplace/b2w)

### Inferences
- **AEMP / ISO 15143-3 is the de facto interoperability layer** for mixed heavy-equipment fleets. CAT, Deere, Komatsu, Volvo, JCB and others are all brands present in Turkey. - **Offline:** competitors offer offline only in their mobile capture apps. Alpnex's offline desktop with SQLite sync is a differentiator for Turkish sites with weak connectivity.

## Q4. What is worth copying for Alpnex, and why

### Takeaway
The highest-value candidates are mostly low-cost refinements of modules Alpnex already has: meter integrity, PM templates and planned/actual calculation, field-request→WO conversion, equipment usage rates charged to sites, and zimmet requests/approvals/audits. Telematics/AEMP import and BLE gateways are higher-cost, later-stage items. All are candidates; competitor presence is not a requirement.

### Cited Findings
Each feature below is listed with its source example, its relevance to Alpnex, and a priority. The priorities are my own judgement.

**1. Meter integrity rules**
- **Source example:** Fleetio blocks decreasing readings, places backdated readings in sequence, uses Void instead of delete, highlights stale meters, and keeps a meter history — [Fleetio Meter overview](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/meter-overview)
- **Why it matters:** protects periodic km/hour maintenance alerts from typing errors.
- **Priority:** High.

**2. Secondary meter per machine**
- **Source example:** Fleetio — [Fleetio Meter overview](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/meter-overview)
- **Why it matters:** fits cranes and trucks with PTO or generator units.
- **Priority:** Medium.

**3. "Require meter to complete WO" setting**
- **Source example:** B2W Maintain — [B2W Feature Overview 2023](https://www.b2wsoftware.com/wp-content/uploads/B2W-Maintain-FeatureOverview_April2023.pdf)
- **Why it matters:** maintenance records are what reset the next due point.
- **Priority:** High.

**4. Planned vs. actual completion for the next due point; PM templates applied to many machines**
- **Source example:** B2W Maintain — [Trimble B2W Maintain](https://www.trimble.com/products/b2w-software/maintain-equipment-maintenance)
- **Why it matters:** fits Alpnex's existing "maintenance definitions" and vehicle templates.
- **Priority:** High.

**5. Alert → work order in a few clicks; field request/defect → work order**
- **Source example:** HCSS — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360); Tenna — [Tenna PM](https://www.tenna.com/construction-equipment-maintenance/preventive-maintenance/)
- **Why it matters:** links Alpnex's existing maintenance alerts and work orders.
- **Priority:** High.

**6. Mechanic time on work orders**
- **Source example:** HCSS — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360)
- **Why it matters:** gives the labor cost of a repair.
- **Priority:** Medium.

**7. Equipment usage rates (hourly/daily/standby) charged to the site**
- **Source example:** Vista Usage Posting — [Trimble Vista help](https://help.trimble.com/doc/vista/vista/job-resources/equipment-management)
- **Why it matters:** gives per-site cost of own and rented machines; Alpnex already stores rental prices.
- **Priority:** High for cost reporting.

**8. Location transfer with attachments moving together**
- **Source example:** Vista — [Trimble Vista help](https://help.trimble.com/doc/vista/vista/job-resources/equipment-management)
- **Why it matters:** an excavator with a breaker hammer, for example, moves as one unit.
- **Priority:** Medium.

**9. Component tracking (engine, transmission)**
- **Source example:** Vista — [Trimble Vista help](https://help.trimble.com/doc/vista/vista/job-resources/equipment-management)
- **Why it matters:** major repair cost history.
- **Priority:** Low–Medium.

**10. Zimmet borrow request → approval → notification; cart bulk check-out; scan-based tool audit**
- **Source example:** ShareMyToolbox — [ShareMyToolbox FAQ](https://www.sharemytoolbox.com/faq/)
- **Why it matters:** improves the existing zimmet module.
- **Priority:** High.

**11. Pick tickets from depot to site; kit templates; low-stock → purchase request**
- **Source example:** ToolWatch — [ToolWatch](https://alignops.com/toolwatch)
- **Why it matters:** fits Alpnex's multi-depot stock model.
- **Priority:** Medium–High.

**12. Audit trail of every transfer and return**
- **Source example:** ToolWatch — [ToolWatch](https://alignops.com/toolwatch)
- **Why it matters:** supports accountability in disputes.
- **Priority:** High.

**13. Certificate/training expiry for operators, plus calibration alerts for tools**
- **Source example:** Hilti — [ON!Track Active Tracking](https://www.hilti.group/content/hilti/CP/XX/en/services/tool-services/on-track/on-track-active-tracking.html); HCSS — [HCSS Equipment360](https://hcss.com/equipment360)
- **Why it matters:** a Turkish operator certificate requirement (e.g. G-class operator licence / MYK certificate) is a plausible use **(unverified, needs domestic research)**.
- **Priority:** Medium.

**14. Fuel auto-void by standard deviation; fuel cost per engine hour; idle fuel; scheduled emailed fuel reports**
- **Source example:** Fleetio — [Fleetio Auto Void](https://help.fleetio.com/en_US/auto-void-fuel-entry); Clue — [Clue Fuel](https://getclue.com/solutions/fuel-management-software)
- **Why it matters:** complements Alpnex's existing deviation alerts.
- **Priority:** Medium.

**15. Dispenser/automation import (tank, refill)**
- **Source example:** Clue PIUSI B.Smart — [Clue Fuel](https://getclue.com/solutions/fuel-management-software)
- **Why it matters:** only relevant if customers use automated dispensers.
- **Priority:** Low.

**16. Telematics import via ISO 15143-3 / OEM feeds (hours, location, fuel, faults)**
- **Source example:** Tenna — [Tenna OEM/AEMP](https://www.tenna.com/integrated-construction-software/oem-telematics-aemp-integrations/); Samsara — [Samsara KB CAT](https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041465972-Integrate-with-Caterpillar-VisionLink)
- **Why it matters:** removes manual meter entry for large fleets.
- **Priority:** Low now, strategic later.

**17. BLE/gateway auto-location with suggested transfers**
- **Source example:** Hilti — [ON!Track suggested transfers](https://help.ontrack3.hilti.com/hc/en-us/articles/34399871008785-How-suggested-transfers-functionality-works)
- **Why it matters:** needs hardware, so it does not fit a no-hardware SaaS today.
- **Priority:** Low.

**18. Equipment request/reservation from site; dispatch board**
- **Source example:** Tenna — [Tenna App](https://www.tenna.com/construction-asset-management/equipment-management-app/); B2W Track/Schedule — [Trimble B2W Track](https://www.trimble.com/en/products/b2w-software/track)
- **Why it matters:** sites request machines from the central fleet.
- **Priority:** Medium.

### Inferences
- **Fits Alpnex's offline desktop:** the high-priority items (1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 12) are pure data and workflow features that work offline and do not depend on hardware or third-party APIs.
- **Do not suit Alpnex's current architecture:** telematics and BLE items need online server-side polling and hardware partnerships. They suit the cloud PostgreSQL side rather than the offline desktop client.
- **Competitive angle:** none of the comparables advertises offline-first desktop operation or Turkish localization, which supports Alpnex's positioning.

### Gaps
- Whether Turkish construction fleets commonly have OEM telematics subscriptions (VisionLink, JDLink, Komtrax, LiveLink) active and AEMP-accessible is **(unverified)**.
- Domestic Turkish competitor equipment modules are out of this note's scope.
- Fleet sizes and pricing that would determine ROI were not researched.
